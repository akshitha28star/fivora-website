YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

FIVORA ("we", "our" or "us") is a digital-products brand. This policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website and business operations. For privacy questions, contact fivoradigital@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

This website is an informational website. It does not currently provide customer accounts, checkout, a contact form or an on-site product purchase facility. If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

To operate our digital-products business, we may process business information such as product ideas and content, product files and designs, listing descriptions, workflow status, approval responses, Pinterest board and Pin information, and business performance figures that we enter or obtain from supported services. We do not intend to collect customer payment-card details through this website.

3. Tools and service providers

FIVORA uses or plans to use the following services for business operations. The exact information processed depends on which integrations are enabled:

  • n8n: to run business automation workflows and connect authorised services.
  • Google Drive: to store generated product files and related business documents.
  • Google Sheets: to maintain product, workflow and business tracking records.
  • Gmail: to send business and product-approval emails.
  • Canva: to create and export product designs and promotional images.
  • Pinterest: to manage our own authorised business account, boards and promotional Pins.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their services may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Please review the relevant provider policies for details.

4. Pinterest access and publishing

If we connect our Pinterest Business account to FIVORA automation, the workflow may access information needed to manage our own authorised account, such as account or board identifiers and Pin content or status. We use this access for FIVORA's own content management and promotion. We do not intend to access other users' private information or sell Pinterest data. Each Pin must be reviewed and explicitly approved by the account owner before it is published.

5. How we use information

We use information to create and manage digital products, prepare product listings and promotional content, operate and troubleshoot workflows, maintain business records, respond to enquiries, and protect the security of our accounts and services.

6. Credentials and security

Where supported, service connections use the relevant provider's authorisation process. Credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored in the relevant service's credential-management system, such as n8n credentials, rather than in public website content. We take reasonable steps to limit access and protect business information. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. We may allow the service providers listed above to process information as needed to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, users or services.

8. Retention and your choices

We keep business records and information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operational, security, accounting and legal needs. You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent directly to us or request correction or deletion. We will consider requests subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. Information held by third-party providers may also need to be managed through those providers.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to invite children to submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, products, automation or service providers change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the date above.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email fivoradigital@gmail.com.

Please note: This policy is a business website draft, not legal advice. Before submitting it for a platform review, FIVORA must verify that every statement matches the services actually connected, the data those services process, the live workflow's approval controls and applicable legal requirements.
